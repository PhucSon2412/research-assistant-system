Figure 3 shows per-species recall; the exact values appear only in the figure. Mean recall fell from 0.71 at 2 lux to 0.52 at 0.5 lux across all nine species. False positives were most often caused by moving branches in windy conditions. Recall was stable across the two recording seasons for most of the species. Small animals such as mice were detected less often than larger mammals. Detection latency on the field hardware stayed below 40 milliseconds per clip. Adding a fifth temporal bin did not change mean recall in our ablation runs. Clips with rain produced more events but did not reduce recall noticeably.
