Methods The forecaster is a linear model over the last 20 temperature readings sampled at 10 Hz. Jobs are deferred when the forecast exceeds 78 degrees Celsius within the next two seconds. Deferred jobs wait in a priority queue and are resumed once the forecast falls again. Interactive requests are never deferred for longer than 300 milliseconds in this design. We evaluated three device types: a phone SoC, a single-board computer and a drone controller. Each device ran the same image classification model with a batch size of one request. Every experiment lasted two hours at an ambient temperature of 24 degrees Celsius. Latency was logged per request, and throttling events were read from the kernel log.
