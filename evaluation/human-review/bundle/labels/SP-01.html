## Methods Each GSR layer contains sixteen feed-forward experts. The gate selects the top-2 experts per token and adds a load-balancing loss with coefficient 0.01. We replace every second feed-forward block of a 6-layer encoder-decoder with a GSR layer. Training used 1.2 million Kinyarwanda–English sentence pairs. Tokenization used a SentencePiece vocabulary of 32,000 pieces trained on the target-side corpus only for the dense baseline and on both sides for GSR.
