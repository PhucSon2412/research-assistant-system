Results Across the three devices, p95 latency fell by 23% relative to first-come-first-served. Throttling events dropped from 41 to 9 per hour on average across the three devices. Energy per inference rose by 4% because deferred jobs later ran at higher clock speeds. The phone SoC showed the largest latency gain, a 31% reduction of p95 latency. The single-board computer improved by 27%, with most gains during long bursts. Median latency changed by less than 2% on every device, so the gain lies in the tail. Forecast error stayed below 1.5 degrees Celsius for 95% of the prediction windows. The scheduler itself used less than 1% of processor time on each of the devices.
