Methods Events are accumulated into 4 temporal bins of 25 milliseconds each. The detector was trained on 14,200 labelled clips from 3 forest sites. Clips were labelled by two ecologists who marked the species and a bounding box. Light levels were measured with a calibrated lux meter placed next to the camera. We report recall because false negatives matter most for population surveys. Each site contributed clips from both summer and winter recording periods. Training used a fixed learning schedule without any per-site tuning at all. Test clips come from recording nights that were not used during training.
