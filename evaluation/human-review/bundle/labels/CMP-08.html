Methods Each hospital ran 5 local epochs per round for 60 rounds of federated training. Updates were clipped to norm 1.0 and aggregated with secure summation. The model is a gradient-boosted ensemble distilled into a small neural network. Features include diagnoses, length of stay, age group and prior admissions. The outcome is an unplanned readmission within 30 days of hospital discharge. Pooled training on the same features serves as an upper reference model. Each hospital held out 20% of its admissions for the final evaluation step. We repeated federated training with three random seeds for every setting.
