6## Abstract
7
8We measure the energy of sparse mixture-of-experts translation inference on a single-board computer.
9
10## Methods
11
12All measurements used a single-board computer with 8 GB of RAM and an external power meter sampling at 1 kHz.
13The sparse model routes each token to two experts, and the dense model has the same number of active parameters.
14
15## Results
16
17The top-2 mixture-of-experts model used 1.3 times the energy per token of the dense model.
18Loading expert weights from flash memory accounted for 58% of the extra energy.
19Caching the four most used experts in RAM reduced the overhead to 1.1 times the dense energy per token.
20
21## Limitations
22
23We measured only one device class and only batch size 1.
