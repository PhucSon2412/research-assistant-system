6## Abstract
7
8We forecast volumetric soil moisture seven days ahead from a network of 212 sensor stations using a graph neural network (GNN).
9The GNN reaches a mean absolute error of 0.031 m3/m3, compared with 0.036 m3/m3 for a physics-based water-balance model.
10
11## Methods
12
13Stations are graph nodes, and edges connect stations that lie within 25 km of each other.
14Inputs are the previous 30 days of moisture, precipitation and air temperature at each station.
15
16## Results
17
18At arid stations the physics-based model remained more accurate, with an error of 0.028 against 0.034 for the GNN.
19The overall GNN advantage came mainly from humid and temperate stations.
