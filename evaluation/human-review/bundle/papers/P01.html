1# Gated Sparse Routing for Low-Resource Machine Translation
2
3Authors: Lena Okafor and Mateo Reyes (fictional authors; synthetic evaluation paper).
4Identifier: doi:10.5555/ras-eval.p01 (fictional, not registered).
5
6## Abstract
7
8We introduce Gated Sparse Routing (GSR), a mixture-of-experts layer that sends each token to a small subset of experts through a learned gate.
9On the Kinyarwanda–English benchmark, GSR improves BLEU by 2.4 points over a dense Transformer baseline with the same number of active parameters.
10
11## Introduction
12
13Low-resource translation models must share capacity between frequent and rare morphology.
14Dense models apply every parameter to every token, which wastes capacity on common words.
15Sparse routing lets rare word forms use specialised experts without increasing the cost per token.
16
17## Methods
18
19Each GSR layer contains sixteen feed-forward experts.
20The gate selects the top-2 experts per token and adds a load-balancing loss with coefficient 0.01.
21We replace every second feed-forward block of a 6-layer encoder-decoder with a GSR layer.
22Training used 1.2 million Kinyarwanda–English sentence pairs.
23Tokenization used a SentencePiece vocabulary of 32,000 pieces trained on the target-side corpus only for the dense baseline and on both sides for GSR.
24
25## Results
26
27GSR reached 27.9 BLEU on the Kinyarwanda–English test set, compared with 25.5 BLEU for the dense baseline.
28On Luganda–English the gain over the dense baseline was 1.3 BLEU points.
29Training took 38 GPU-hours for GSR, against 29 GPU-hours for the dense baseline.
30
31## Analysis
32
33Expert utilisation stayed within 4% of uniform after the first 5,000 updates.
34Removing the load-balancing loss caused two experts to receive 61% of all tokens and reduced BLEU by 0.9 points.
35
36## Limitations
37
38GSR lost 1.8 BLEU on code-switched Kinyarwanda–English input, where the gate routed most mixed-language tokens to a single expert.
39We did not evaluate languages outside the Bantu family.
40
41## Conclusion
42
43Gated Sparse Routing is a simple way to add capacity for rare morphology in low-resource translation.
