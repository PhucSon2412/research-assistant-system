6## Abstract
7
8We forecast daily electricity demand for rural microgrids across two monsoon seasons.
9
10## Methods
11
12A gradient-boosted tree model uses calendar features, temperature and the previous 14 days of load.
13The dataset covers 36 microgrids with between 40 and 900 connected households.
14
15## Results
16
17The model forecast daily load with a mean absolute percentage error of 8.4%.
18Errors were largest during the first week of each monsoon season.
