6## Abstract
7
8We replicate Gated Sparse Routing on Kinyarwanda–English using the published configuration.
9We observe a gain of 0.6 BLEU over the dense baseline, much smaller than the 2.4 points originally reported.
10
11## Methods
12
13We used sixteen experts with top-2 routing and a load-balancing coefficient of 0.01, matching the original description.
14Unlike the original study, we trained both models with one unigram tokenizer of 32,000 pieces learned on the combined source and target corpus.
15Both models were trained for the same number of updates on the 1.2 million sentence pairs.
16
17## Results
18
19The dense baseline scored 26.8 BLEU and the routed model scored 27.4 BLEU.
20When we re-ran the baseline with the tokenizer settings reported for the original baseline, it dropped to 25.6 BLEU.
21
22## Discussion
23
24We attribute most of the original gap to a tokenizer mismatch between the baseline and the routed model rather than to routing itself.
25We did not have access to the original checkpoints, so other differences cannot be ruled out.
