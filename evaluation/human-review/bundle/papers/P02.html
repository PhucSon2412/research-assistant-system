6## Abstract
7
8Dense Adapter Stacks (DAS) insert small bottleneck adapters into every layer of a frozen multilingual model.
9DAS improves Kinyarwanda–English BLEU by 1.1 points while updating only 3% of the parameters.
10
11## Introduction
12
13Full fine-tuning of large multilingual models is expensive for low-resource language pairs.
14Adapters keep the base model frozen and train a small number of new weights.
15
16## Methods
17
18The base model is a 12-layer multilingual encoder-decoder that stays frozen during training.
19Each layer receives two adapters of rank 64, one after attention and one after the feed-forward block.
20We trained on the same 1.2 million Kinyarwanda–English sentence pairs used in prior routing work.
21Optimisation settings follow the base model release and are not tuned for this study.
22
23## Results
24
25DAS scored 26.6 BLEU, against 25.5 BLEU for the dense baseline, a gain of 1.1 points.
26Training required 21 GPU-hours on the same hardware class as the baseline.
27On code-switched input DAS improved by 0.8 BLEU over the baseline instead of degrading.
28
29## Efficiency
30
31The adapters add 3% trainable parameters and 5% inference latency at batch size 1.
32Memory use during training was 40% lower than full fine-tuning.
33
34## Limitations
35
36The DAS gain shrinks to 0.2 BLEU when more than 5 million sentence pairs are available.
37We report a single random seed per configuration.
