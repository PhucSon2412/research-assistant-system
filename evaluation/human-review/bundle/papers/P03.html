1# CNDI-5k: A Benchmark for Clinical Note De-identification

6## Abstract
7
8CNDI-5k contains 5,000 synthetic clinical notes annotated for 11 categories of protected health information.
9We release baselines for span-level de-identification.
10
11## Annotation
12
13Three annotators labelled every note, and inter-annotator agreement reached a Cohen's kappa of 0.87.
14Notes were generated from templates and contain no real patient data.
15Disagreements were resolved by a fourth senior annotator.
16
17## Results
18
19A hybrid model combining a conditional random field with a BERT encoder reached a span-level F1 of 0.912.
20A rule-based baseline reached a span-level F1 of 0.744.
21Dates and phone numbers were the easiest categories, while free-text occupations were the hardest at F1 0.61.
22
23## Access
24
25The dataset is released under a research-only licence that forbids re-identification attempts.
26Access requires a signed data use agreement.
27
28## Limitations
29
30All notes are in English, and template generation may understate the variety of real clinical writing.
