6## Abstract
7
8We order training batches from short, frequent sentences to long sentences with rare words.
9Curriculum sampling improves Kinyarwanda–English BLEU by 1.7 points and Swahili–English BLEU by 0.9 points.
10
11## Methods
12
13Sentences are scored by length and by the mean log-frequency of their tokens.
14The curriculum spans the first 40% of training steps, after which batches are sampled uniformly.
15We trained on 1.2 million Kinyarwanda–English pairs and 2.0 million Swahili–English pairs.
16
17## Results
18
19The curriculum model reached 27.2 BLEU on Kinyarwanda–English against a baseline of 25.5 BLEU.
20It reached convergence 18% sooner in wall-clock time than uniform sampling.
21On Swahili–English, BLEU improved from 31.0 to 31.9.
22
23## Limitations
24
25Curriculum sampling had no measurable effect on code-switched input, with a change of 0.1 BLEU.
26It requires a frequency table computed from the full training set before training starts.
