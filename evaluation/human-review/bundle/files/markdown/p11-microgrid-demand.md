# Seasonal Demand Forecasting for Rural Microgrids

Authors: Nomvula Dube and Karl Brandt (fictional authors; synthetic evaluation paper).
Identifier: doi:10.5555/ras-eval.p11 (fictional, not registered).

## Abstract

We forecast daily electricity demand for rural microgrids across two monsoon seasons.

## Methods

A gradient-boosted tree model uses calendar features, temperature and the previous 14 days of load.
The dataset covers 36 microgrids with between 40 and 900 connected households.

## Results

The model forecast daily load with a mean absolute percentage error of 8.4%.
Errors were largest during the first week of each monsoon season.
