## Abstract

We measure how much a byte-pair tokenizer changes when it is re-learned during continual pretraining.

## Results

Tokenizer drift, measured as the share of new merges, reached 7.5% after 40 billion tokens of continual pretraining.
Drift was concentrated in code and chemical names.
