## Abstract

We apply gated sparse routing to acoustic models for low-resource speech recognition.

## Methods

Each routed layer holds 32 experts, and the gate selects the top-4 experts per audio frame.

## Results

Gated sparse routing reduced the word error rate to 7.9% on a Kinyarwanda speech corpus, from 9.6% for a dense acoustic model.
